字義與讀音

「郭」字本義為外城，即古代城牆外圍再加築的一道城牆。後引申為城郭、外圍之意。

讀音因方言而異：普通話 Guō、粵語 Kwok / Gwok3。香港及海外粵語社群多以 Kwok 為姓氏拼寫。

得姓起源

郭姓是中華古老大姓之一，起源多元，主要有以下幾支：

一、姬姓 · 虢國（主流）

源於周王室宗支。周武王分封叔父虢仲於西虢、虢叔於東虢。因「虢」與「郭」古音相通，虢國後裔遂以郭為氏。太原郭氏即由此而來。

二、任姓 · 古郭國

源於黃帝後裔。夏商時期建立古郭國，國滅後子孫以國為氏。此支歷史可追溯至四千多年前。

三、以居邑為氏

居住於城郭附近的人，以「郭」為姓。亦有東郭、南郭等複姓，後多簡化為單姓郭。

四、其他來源

包括少數民族改姓、賜姓等。例如後周太祖郭威本姓常，隨母改姓郭。

The Kwok surname is one of the ancient major Chinese clans with multiple origins:

1. Ji Surname · State of Guo (Main Line)

Descended from the Zhou royal house. Because “虢” and “郭” were homophonous, descendants adopted 郭 as their surname. The famous Taiyuan Kwok clan originated from this branch.

2. Ren Surname · Ancient Guo State

From descendants of the Yellow Emperor. An ancient Guo state existed during Xia and Shang. After its fall, people took the state name as surname. This line can be traced back over 4,000 years.

3. From Place of Residence

People living near the outer city walls adopted 郭 as their surname. Compound surnames such as Dongguo later simplified to Kwok.

4. Other Origins

Including name changes and bestowals. For example, Emperor Taizu of Later Zhou was originally surnamed Chang.

The Kwok surname ranks among the 16th–18th most common surnames in contemporary China, with around 15 million people. It is concentrated in Henan, Hebei, Shandong and Guangdong. In Hong Kong, Kwok is a common surname.

Large numbers of descendants live in Southeast Asia, North America, Australia and other parts of the Chinese diaspora.
